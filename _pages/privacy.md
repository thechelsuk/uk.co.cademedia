## The short version

This site doesn't use accounts, forms, analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't collect or sell your personal data.

## What this site stores

- **Your theme choice.** If you pick light or dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it sticks between visits. It never leaves your device. Choosing "System" removes it.

That's it. There are no tracking cookies set by this site.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as your IP address) when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Things we embed or link to

Some pages load content from other services. When they do, those services may set cookies or collect data under their own policies:

- **YouTube** videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`), which doesn't store viewing information until you play a video. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **TikTok** clips use TikTok's embedded player, which may ask for cookie consent. [TikTok's privacy policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en).
- **Podcast audio** streams from our podcast host (Megaphone) when you press play.
- **Fonts** load from Google Fonts. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Sponsored posts** link to our sponsors' sites. We don't share any information with sponsors.

Links to Patreon, our merch shop, social networks and other sites are covered by those sites' own policies.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over your personal data. Because this site doesn't collect any, there's usually nothing for us to share or delete — but if you have a question, email [{{ site.legal.email }}](mailto:{{ site.legal.email }}).

You can also contact the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
